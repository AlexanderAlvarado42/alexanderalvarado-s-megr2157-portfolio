## Objective
The objective of this assignment is for the student to create a professional engineering portfolio that shows their ability to analyze problems, make decisions, and explain their reasoning. They will do this by analyzing other engineering portfolios, studying a simple mechanical product, and making decisions about how their own portfolio should be organized. The goal is to create a portfolio that clearly shows their engineering skills and can be used throughout the semester.
